## 2. 🔤 The 3 Fonts (Free on Google Fonts)

| # | Font Family | Purpose / Where to Use | Weights to Use | Google Font Link |
| :-: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Bricolage Grotesque** | All Headings, Titles & Big Numbers | **Bold (700)**, **ExtraBold (800)** | [Download Font](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Bricolage+Grotesque) |
| **2** | **Instrument Sans** | Body Copy, UI, Buttons & Navigation | **Regular (400)**, **Bold (700)** | [Download Font](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Sans) |
| **3** | **Instrument Serif** | Emotional Quotes & Testimonials | **Italic (400i)** | [Download Font](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Serif) |

> 💡 **Button Style Rule:** Action buttons always use **rounded pill shape (fully round)** with **12px UPPERCASE bold text** and letter spacing.

## 3. 📐 Font Sizes & Hierarchy

| Role / Heading | Font Size | Font Family & Weight | Example / Use Case |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Hero Main Title** | **48px – 64px** | Bricolage Grotesque · **ExtraBold (800)** | Top homepage cover title |
| **Heading 1 (H1)** | **36px – 40px** | Bricolage Grotesque · **Bold (700)** | Main section headline |
| **Heading 2 (H2)** | **28px – 32px** | Bricolage Grotesque · **Bold (700)** | Program & project titles |
| **Heading 3 (H3)** | **20px – 24px** | Bricolage Grotesque · **Bold (700)** | Card titles & subheaders |
| **Big Numbers / Stats** | **48px** | Bricolage Grotesque · **ExtraBold (800)** | Metrics (e.g. *50,000+ Bags*) |
| **Quotes & Testimonials** | **24px – 28px** | Instrument Serif · **Italic (400i)** | Reflective community quotes |
| **Body Paragraphs** | **16px** | Instrument Sans · **Regular (400)** | Standard reading text |
| **Small / Metadata** | **14px** | Instrument Sans · **Regular (400)** | Dates, secondary descriptions |
| **Buttons & Badges** | **12px (ALL CAPS)** | Instrument Sans · **Bold (700)** | Pill CTA buttons & chips |
