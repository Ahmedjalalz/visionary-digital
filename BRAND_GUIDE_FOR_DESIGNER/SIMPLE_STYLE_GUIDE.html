🔤 The 3 Fonts (Google Fonts)

1. Bricolage Grotesque

Use for: Headings, Hero titles, and Big Numbers · Weights: Bold (700), ExtraBold (800)
The Quick Brown Fox Jumps Over The Lazy Dog 12345

2. Instrument Sans

Use for: Body copy, UI buttons, and navigation · Weights: Regular (400), Medium (500), Bold (700)
The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog 12345

3. Instrument Serif

Use for: Emotional quotes and testimonials · Weight: Regular (400), Italic (400i)
“Every child seated with pencil and paper has their future restored.”
💡 Buttons & Labels: Always use rounded pill buttons (full radius) with uppercase bold text (12px) and spaced letters.
