03. The Typography System — Editorial Trio
Three complementary Google Fonts calibrated for structure, clarity, and emotion
Display & Headings

Bricolage Grotesque

Authoritative neo-grotesque with expressive humanist curves. Used for hero titles, section headlines, and big tabular metrics.

SemiBold 600 Bold 700 ExtraBold 800
Body, Navigation & UI

Instrument Sans

Uncompromising modern geometric clarity with OpenType alternates (ss01, cv01). Optimized for prolonged reading and dense UI.

Regular 400 Medium 500 SemiBold 600 Bold 700
Emotive & Editorial Accent

Instrument Serif

Refined high-contrast transitional serif. Used for heartfelt pull quotes, emotional human narratives, and philosophical statements.
